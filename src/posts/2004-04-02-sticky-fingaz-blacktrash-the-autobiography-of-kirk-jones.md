I first heard Sticky Fingaz on Eminem's "The Marshall Mather's LP" on track 9: "Remember Me?" . That is one of my favorite songs on that CD, so I thought I'd check out Sticky's solo work. Come to find out, he is, or was anyway, half of Onyx. I was never an Onyx fan, so that didn't really have any impact on the thoughts I have for this CD. The CD tells a story, actually an autobiography, with small skits connecting the songs. Eminem is featured on track 15: "What if I was White?". It's funny how the titles for the songs Em & Sticky do together always present these questions that require utmost thought and synthesis. Other notable appearances include: Raekwon and Redman.
