This is the only album I have found by them on which I like most of the songs. I have heard most of their other albums, and, to say the least, I'm not impressed. There is one song on this album I don't care for (track 6: Clove Smoke Catharsis) and most of their newer music sounds similar to that song. It sounds more like the whiny, commercial, pop-punk you would hear on Mtv or the radio. Track 1 is an intro song, track 4 is my favorite with track 2 being next.
