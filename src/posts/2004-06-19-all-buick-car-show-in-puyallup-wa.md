<p class="note"><em>From my journal, June 19, 2004:</em> I added pictures I took at Puyallup's Buick car show today. It is still hot here. I'm amazed it hasn't started raining yet because it has been raining off and on all summer so far. I got an inflatable boat at Walmart yesterday for our upcoming camping trip. I'm really excited for the trip. I am still looking for one of those red Mariners hats with the white S on them. I have been to every store in the South Hill and Tacoma malls. I have spent countless hours scouring the internet and ebay. If you know where to find them, please let me know in the forum.</p>

I went to this car show with my dad. It was really hot. I took these with Cathryn's Sony DSC-P52. I pretty much hate my Toshiba PDR-M25 after using the Sony. There are some pictures of the train station area at the end.
