The first actual song on this CD, track 2: Bank to Bank, has one of the sickest beats I have heard in a while. That's no surprise considering Eligh produced it and most of the other tracks on the CD (2-4, 9, 12, 14). Other production credits go to: Mums the Word (5, 8, 13), Slant (6, 15), The Grouch (7), and Champ(10, 11). The intro doesn't need someone to produce it, I guess, because it's just a recording of someone talking to a woman.
