Not much is new for the beginning of October. I'm excited for J5 coming to town with Planet Asia and Skillz on the 29th. Also the Beatnuts and Non Phixion will be at the I-Spy in Seattle on the 12th. I saw the CMA, Bicasso, DJ Murge and Son Doobie at I-Spy this summer.
