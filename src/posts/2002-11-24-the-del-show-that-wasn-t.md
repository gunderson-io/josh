The Del show did not go as planned. While waiting in line outside (way after the doors were supposed to have been open) I passed out and collapsed. We decided it was better not to go to the show and sold our tickets. Luckily we did because my friend said Del didn't even come out. When I said &quot;Del is going to be sick&quot; I didn't mean it in the literal sense. I would have been pissed if we had gone in and he didn't come out. I'm sure the People Under the Stairs would have been cool but as for the rest of the bunch, I would have rather sat in AC Circuit Analysis and battled Ash.
