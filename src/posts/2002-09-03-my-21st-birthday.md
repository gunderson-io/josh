What I can remember drinking was several beers, several rum &amp; cokes, several shots of <a href="http://www.jager.com">Jäger</a>, a "Blowjob", a tequila and tabasco, a cement mixer, and some blue shot chased by (I think) <a href="http://www.bacardi.com">Bacardi Limón</a> &amp; Sprite. On Monday my uncle brought me a beer and I took one drink and then puked up the dissolved pills I took earlier to relieve some of my head pressure. Feeling a lot better today.
