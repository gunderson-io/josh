Here's an actual update to the main page at long last. I was trying to make the snowflakes in
the background scroll downward but the DOCTYPE tag at the top of these pages that say this is
XHTML interfere with the JavaScript. If I took the tag out, the snowflakes scroll. I'm not sure
how taking that out will affect my pages so I decided it wasn't worth it. I think it has
something to do with how the DOM is accessed with XHTML.
