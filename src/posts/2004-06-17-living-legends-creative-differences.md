Productions by Eligh, Grouch, Scarub, Hektic, Bicasso, Sunspot, and the A.M. allow for a diverse presentation. I've never heard of Hektic or the A.M., but the Asop track that Hektic produced (track 7: Awakening) is one of my favorite songs on here. The horns and whatnot sound like a '70s TV show or something. I really like the title track (produced by Eligh) and the second track (Fill My Drink Up, produced by Grouch). I was lucky enough to get this with the limited edition LL 101 CD and a poster.
