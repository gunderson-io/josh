I have already purchased my ticket for the Del show next Sunday at the Showbox. People Under the Stairs, Motion Man, Kutmasta Kurt and Lifesavas will also be there. I saw Lifesavas at EMP's Sky Church with Blackalicious last year, I think. They were kind of whack. Del is going to be sick. I saw something strange in a recent e-mail: Swollen Members and Sweatshop Union will be performing with Snoop Dogg in Kamloops, BC in December. I don't know how many of the 'heads will be into the whole gangsta thing...but if it's anything like Seattle, it'll just be a bunch of white kids who don't care.
